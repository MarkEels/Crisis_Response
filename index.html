Duty and Call Expectations
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD) are REQUIRED to respond on-site in person:
  • RA requests or requires backup, following assessment
  • Serious roommate conflicts, may require resident relocation
  • Concerns of excessive alcohol intoxication / unresponsiveness
  • Serious illness or urgent health situations
  • Sensitive mental health concern
  • Check-in requested by Wellness Exchange or NYU Shanghai Counselor on Duty
  • Physical altercation
  • Sexual misconduct, domestic violence, or dating violence
  • Student is in the hospital with a life-threatening condition
  • Fire or facilities issue requiring extensive repairs and/or resident relocation
  • Coordinated search by Campus Safety or local Police
  • In consultation with the Administrator on Duty (AOD)
Professional Staff on Duty are REQUIRED to contact the Administrator on Duty:
The Administrator on Duty is a resource to ensure PSODs have the support needed to provide the best possible service to students in crisis.

Contact the AOD for: any in-person response; any unresolved or escalating incident; any health or safety concern; when medical transport is required; when a student is in hospital; any incident that may draw media attention; emergency room change cases of a sensitive nature; uncertainty about how to respond.

Basics of Crisis Management — The ABCD Framework
A
Assess the Situation
Use all your senses to quickly understand what's happening. Ask calm, clear questions. If available, get additional details from roommates, friends, or bystanders.
B
Backup
You are never alone in a crisis. Available resources: RA on Duty, RA on Call, PSOD, AOD, Wellness Exchange, NYU Shanghai Counselor on Duty, Campus Safety, Local Police, and more as needed.
C
Calm Yourself and Others
People take cues from your body language and tone. Take a deep breath. Move slowly and with purpose. Speak in a clear, steady voice — loud enough to be heard, but not shouting. Give simple, direct instructions.
D
Disperse / De-escalate / Debrief
Politely ask onlookers to leave. Stay respectful and composed. Collect information, show courtesy, submit incident report by 9 AM the next business day. Do not speak to media.
Hospital Transport — Excessive Alcohol Consumption
Objective: Prioritize the immediate health and safety of the resident over conduct concerns. Maintain a calm, non-judgmental demeanor. Ensure appropriate medical professionals take over care promptly. Protect the resident's privacy.

CUSP Signs: C – Cold, clammy, pale or bluish skin · U – Unresponsive, mental confusion · S – Slow or shallow breathing · P – Puking
Students should not be left alone.
RA / First Responder
  • Collect resident's name, N number or NetID, location, short assessment
  • Immediately contact Campus Safety if ambulance is needed
  • Immediately call Professional Staff on Duty and follow their instructions
  • Obtain HTH/GeoBlue Guarantee of Payment (GOP) (021-2059-5370), if applicable
  • Record: Hospital name, time, diagnosis (if given), family/friends present
  • If required, accompany the student to the hospital until Pro Staff arrives
  • Stay with the resident until PSOD, AOD, or guardians arrive
  • Connect with Pro Staff on Duty to submit incident report in Advocate by 9 AM next business day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Go on-site to assess the situation in person
  • Document: RA name, hospital name, medical condition
  • Notify AOD for all hospital visits (immediately if serious or life-threatening)
  • Create communication plan with AOD for regular updates
  • Obtain HTH/GeoBlue GOP (021-2059-5370) if applicable
  • If hospital visit needed, accompany the student to hospital in the ambulance
  • Relieve RA when appropriate
  • If student to remain in hospital, send email to shanghai.health@nyu.edu by 9 AM next day
  • When student returns to campus, check-in to offer further support and follow-up
DO NOT: Leave hospital without AOD approval · Contact resident's family / guardians · Sign hospital forms on behalf of the student
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Contact Dean of Students for major hospital transports
  • Verify RA / PSOD steps were completed
  • In consultation with Dean of Students, contact resident's guardians if situation is life-threatening, student is unresponsive, or student is a minor
  • If contacting guardians, share only: reason ("alcohol consumption" or "medical emergency"), hospital name and contact info

  • GeoBlue Insurance: Call GeoBlue for GOP (021-2059-5370); request GeoBlue to send HTH/GeoBlue GOP to hospital and cc to student email
  • On the way to the hospital, call the hospital to inform them of the student's arrival

Arrest of a Student
Objective: Although we are not able to intervene in police matters, being arrested may cause trauma. Staff should gather as much information as possible and provide support. Note: Police have 72 hours to investigate and decide whether to charge or release. During this window, university may not be able to communicate directly with the individual.
RA / First Responder
  • Collect student's name, N#/NetID; details of arrest (when, where, why); emotional condition of student
  • Determine where the student is being taken
  • Contact Professional Staff on Duty
  • Create Advocate report and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Contact the Administrator on Duty
  • Contact Campus Safety
  • Contact the police precinct regarding what the student should expect at 9 AM next morning
  • If possible, speak with the student and ask if they would like anyone to be notified
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Contact the Dean of Students
  • Serve as primary point of contact; talk to student if possible and provide information and support
  • Contact the student's emergency contact and inform them of the status and location of the student
  • Contact the student's respective embassy/consulate for assistance
  • Contact Campus Relations if the arrest may be covered in media
  • Determine whether the arrest necessitates an interim suspension from housing
Assault
Objective: Ensure the safety of the student and provide support and resources to the victim. If the situation is not controlled, call Campus Safety for backup.
RA / First Responder
  • Conduct basic assessment; collect student information (NYU Shanghai ID for N#/NetID)
  • Contact Campus Safety and Professional Staff on Duty
  • Determine if medical assistance needed — if so, refer to Hospital Transport (Urgent Care)
  • Create detailed Advocate report and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Respond to the scene or hospital; contact Administrator on Duty and Campus Safety
  • Determine if reports have been filed with Campus Safety and local police
  • If perpetrator is known, determine if PNG (Persona Non Grata) is possible from victim's residence hall
  • If perpetrator is a student, advise victim of the student conduct process
  • Offer to connect the victim to the Wellness Exchange for support
  • Collect: exactly what happened, when/where, history of threats/conflicts, relationship between parties, whether either student appears under the influence
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Notify the Dean of Students
  • Confer with Dean of Students regarding contacting Campus Relations
  • Determine if it is necessary to send a memo to the community
  • Discuss student conduct options with Director of Community Standards if perpetrator is a student
Bias Incident
Definition: Harassment or intimidation of an individual based on a legally protected characteristic, including race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital/parental status, national origin, ethnicity, citizenship status, veteran status, age, disability, or any other legally protected basis. May be anonymous or directed, or written, verbal, or physical in nature.
RA / First Responder
  • Conduct basic assessment; collect student information (name, N#/NetID)
  • Contact Professional Staff on Duty immediately
  • If graffiti or physical evidence is involved: DO NOT remove it until documented (photographed) by Residential Life Staff
  • Cover graffiti with paper until permanently removed
  • If possible, take photos of the bias incident and include with Advocate report
  • Create detailed Advocate report and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Respond to scene to take photographs; provide support and/or referrals
  • Contact Campus Safety and Administrator on Duty
  • Offer referral to students to Wellness Exchange or Counseling Center
  • If graffiti, contact Facility Management to remove as soon as possible
  • By next day: draft a letter to the affected community — must be reviewed by AOD before sending
  • Ensure Floor RA is aware and prepared to facilitate a floor meeting
  • Consult with appropriate campus resources for follow-up or programming (Health Promotions, Counseling, Center for Student Belonging, etc.)
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Notify the Dean of Students by 9 AM the next day, via email
  • Ensure that all of the above steps have taken place
Suspicion of Controlled Substance
RA / First Responder
  • Begin taking notes of what you see (smells, sounds through doorway, etc.)
  • Call Professional Staff on Duty, explain the situation, and wait on scene for arrival — DO NOT leave the scene
  • If student opens the door, share that you are an RA and that Pro Staff is arriving soon
  • DO NOT begin the search · DO NOT touch any illegal substances
  • Don't put yourself between the student and the exit — if student insists on leaving, let them leave and document
  • Create Advocate report and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Report to the scene and take control
  • Lead search of room that doesn't require touching student's personal items
  • Call AOD if a more thorough search is required
  • Follow Controlled Substance Discovery protocol if substance is discovered
  • DO NOT touch any illegal substances
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Determine if additional search is needed in conference with Dean of Students
Controlled Substance Discovery
RA / First Responder
  • Contact Professional Staff on Duty and Campus Safety immediately
  • Do not touch the substance and/or any drug paraphernalia
  • Take photos of evidence and copious notes (photos of items only, not students)
  • Gather: Student N#/NetID; full names of Campus Safety Officers
  • If student becomes aggressive, de-escalate and maintain calm
  • Don't put yourself between student and exit — if student insists on leaving, let them leave and document
  • Create Advocate report and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Arrive on scene and take control
  • Take photo evidence of what was found and where (items only, not students)
  • Do not touch the substance and/or any drug paraphernalia
  • Call Campus Safety / AOD for instructions on how to/if to dispose of the substance
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Contact Dean of Students if an arrest is made by local Shanghai police
  • Confer with Dean of Students regarding contacting Campus Relations
  • Determine if it is necessary to send a memo to the community
  • Discuss student conduct options with Director of University Community Standards if perpetrator is a student
Death of a Student
Objective: If someone reports the immediate death of a student, have them call Campus Safety immediately, 110 (police) and 120 (ambulance). Pro Staff's role is to contact appropriate staff, respond to the site immediately, set up a command center, and help calm residents.
RA / First Responder
  • Immediately contact Campus Safety, 110 for police, and 120 for ambulance
  • Immediately contact Professional Staff on Duty and wait until they arrive on scene
  • DO NOT disturb the scene at all — secure the scene to prevent any touching of the body or items in the room/area
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Respond to the location immediately
  • Confirm identity of student: name, N Number or NetID
  • Contact AOD for further instruction and Campus Safety for additional information on scene
  • DO NOT release any information to anyone — including media personnel
  • Comfort and relocate impacted individuals; block off or close off the scene
  • Contact Student Health Center team — get a Counselor on scene ASAP
  • Set up a command center in the Office of Student Affairs
  • Ensure that impacted residents have received information about the Student Health Center
  • Create Advocate report and submit by 9 AM next day
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Contact the Dean of Students immediately and respond to the location
  • Confer with Dean of Students regarding notification of family
  • Identify other spaces as needed to temporarily relocate occupants of the room
  • Draft a letter to the community for the Dean of Students to review
  • Meet with all responding Student Affairs staff to provide further instruction and/or support
  • Notify university leadership
Elevator Malfunction
RA / First Responder
  • If possible, determine location of elevator and go to that floor
  • Determine if elevator is occupied and number of people inside
  • Determine if anyone needs medical attention
  • Contact Facility Management and Campus Safety; contact Pro Staff on Duty
  • Stay in contact with occupants if possible — keep them calm
  • Advise those inside the elevator NOT to attempt to get out
  • DO NOT attempt to assist people out of the elevator
  • File detailed Advocate report and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Advise NYUSH Facility Management of the occurrence
  • Follow up with persons that were in the elevator
  • Send a brief summary email to Dean of Students

RA / First Responder

Medical Emergency
Objective: Ensure the safety of the student and seek immediate medical assistance if needed. If a serious emergency, call Campus Safety and 120. Provide emotional support to the injured student and address needs of other students involved.
RA / First Responder
  • Contact Campus Safety and have them call an ambulance (or call 120 if you speak Mandarin) if situation is a serious emergency (unresponsive, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, seizure, head injury, etc.) or patient is unable to walk
  • Contact Professional Staff on Duty, who will determine if resident needs hospital
  • If Pro Staff cannot get to scene, RA will accompany student to hospital — call PSOD when in ambulance to confirm hospital location
  • Create detailed incident report in Advocate and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Immediately report to the scene — if hospital visit necessary, accompany student to SinoUnited Hospital or Shanghai East Hospital
  • Contact AOD
  • Gather from hospital: condition of student, expected length of stay
  • Determine if family notification is needed
  • If student is admitted, contact the Student Health Center team
  • Notify Student Health Center via email of student's status by 9 AM next day (admitted or not)
  • Follow up with resident the next day
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Contact Dean of Students if illness/injury is life-threatening
  • After consulting with Dean of Students, notify the family if required
  • If student will be in hospital for a longer period, determine a follow-up plan
Missing Student
Objective: Gather information, make attempts to locate the student, and notify staff.
RA / First Responder
  • Collect basic information from students, roommates, etc.
  • Call Professional Staff on Duty
  • Create detailed incident report in Advocate and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Find out as much as possible from roommates, friends, and/or the RA from the resident's floor
  • Gather: resident name/N#/NetID; what were they wearing; mood when last seen; did they take anything from their room (suitcase, toothbrush, clothes)
  • Contact Campus Safety to check swipe card records
  • Contact AOD for next steps
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Verify that Campus Safety has been notified
  • Provide additional directives for Professional Staff on Duty
  • If necessary, contact the resident's guardians
  • Discuss parental notification with Dean of Students before notifying
Power Outage
RA / First Responder
  • Determine the extent of the outage
  • Notify Campus Safety and Professional Staff on Duty
  • Check elevators — if people are stuck, notify Campus Safety right away
  • Use flashlights (located with Campus Safety in each tower) to do community walks — remind residents to stay calm and not use candles
  • Create detailed incident report in Advocate and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Contact AOD
  • If people are stuck in elevator, refer to elevator malfunction protocol
  • Ensure RAs are doing community walks and ensuring safety of residents
  • Coordinate with Campus Safety to determine if the fire alarm system is working and organize a fire watch if needed
  • Establish a central communications point
  • Establish communication with on-site facilities staff
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Contact Dean of Students immediately
  • Identify, as needed, a location to be used as an emergency gathering location or temporary shelter
Psychological Emergency: Self-Injurious Behavior
Objective: Any indications that a student is engaging or potentially engaging in self-injurious behavior (biting, hitting, scratching, cutting, burning, banging head against wall) affecting their safety or the health and safety of surrounding students and staff. Contact Wellness Exchange and Campus Safety for immediate emergencies. Note that students who may have witnessed the behavior may be impacted and will need to be followed up with and supported.
RA / First Responder
  • If medical attention is required immediately, contact Campus Safety immediately or 120 (if you speak Mandarin)
  • If urgent, the RA should accompany the student to the hospital — call Pro Staff on Duty when in the ambulance to confirm location
  • If not urgent, collect some basic information and call Pro Staff on Duty
  • Create detailed incident report in Advocate and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • If urgent and student is being transported, accompany to hospital; remind resident to bring Passport or National ID; relieve RA from hospital
  • If not urgent: contact Wellness Exchange (identify yourself as NYU Shanghai staff; provide N#; provide your callback number; provide brief overview of the situation); connect resident with Wellness Exchange representative
  • If resident refuses to speak with Wellness Exchange, encourage them and inform the representative of their resistance
  • Contact AOD and provide overview of the situation
  • Call NYU Shanghai Counselor on Duty to discuss response logistics
  • If student is not transported, check in with resident after a day
  • Provide frequent updates to NYU Shanghai Counselor on Duty
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Assist NYU Shanghai Counselor and on-site staff as needed
  • Contact student's parents if needed (early notification is necessary if police are involved or if ambulance/medical services are required)
  • Ensure ongoing assessment between Pro Staff / Dean of Students / NYU Shanghai Counselor of appropriateness of counseling intervention when emergency treatment/transport is delayed
  • Consult with the Dean of Students
  • If student is admitted: place temporary PNG at student's residence hall until student completes one post-hospital evaluation with Student Health Center
  • If student is not admitted: mandate a two-part risk assessment
  • Notify Dean of Students via email of student contact regardless of hospitalization by 9 AM next business day
Psychological Emergency: Suicidal Ideation
Objective: Any indications that a student has suicidal ideation (talking about death and dying, talking about harming/killing oneself, giving possessions away). Contact Wellness Exchange and Campus Safety for immediate emergencies. Students who may have witnessed the behavior may be profoundly impacted and will need to be followed up with and supported.
RA / First Responder
  • If medical attention is required immediately, contact Campus Safety immediately or 120 (if you speak Mandarin)
  • Collect basic information and call Pro Staff on Duty
  • If urgent, RA can accompany the student to the hospital — call PSOD when in ambulance to confirm hospital location
  • Create detailed incident report in Advocate and submit by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Report to the scene immediately and briefly assess
  • If medical attention required, accompany resident to hospital; remind resident to take Passport or National ID
  • Notify AOD
  • If no immediate medical attention required: connect student to Wellness Exchange; if student reluctant, be encouraging and tell them about the benefits of speaking with a wellness professional
  • When calling Wellness Exchange: identify yourself as NYU Shanghai staff; provide N#; provide callback number; provide brief overview; note if student is reluctant to talk
  • Wellness Exchange may suggest contacting NYU Shanghai Counselor on Duty to discuss response logistics
  • If student is not transported, have a staff member check in with the resident later
  • Provide frequent updates to NYU Shanghai Counselor on Duty
  • Submit IR by 9 AM next business day
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Assist NYU Shanghai Counselor on Duty and on-site staff as needed
  • Contact student's parents as needed
  • Ensure ongoing assessment between Pro Staff / Admin on Duty / NYU Shanghai Counselor
  • Consult with Dean of Students; Dean may follow up with responding RAs and residents the next day
  • If student is admitted: place temporary PNG at student's residence hall until post-hospital evaluation with Student Health Center
  • If student is not admitted: mandate a two-part risk assessment
  • Follow up with Dean of Students via email by 9 AM next business day
Special Note: If a resident shares that they may have been depressed, possibly suicidal, or hospitalized in the distant past (e.g. during a BASE interview), the RA must share this with their supervisor. As long as there is no indication of a present crisis, the RA need not disclose the identity of the resident. Discuss with supervisor to identify possible triggers and red flags to look for.

Sexual Assault
Objective: Ensure the safety of and provide support and resources to the survivor. Speak softly and gently. Communicate an attitude of acceptance and caring. Be non-judgmental. Survivors may have an aversion to physical contact — ask for permission to touch. Emphasize you are there to help. Assure the survivor they are not alone.
RA / First Responder
  • Listen to the resident and provide emotional support; speak softly and gently; be non-judgmental
  • Gently inform the survivor that you are required to notify Professional Staff on Duty immediately
  • Inform the survivor that PSOD can walk them through all options and help facilitate connections with supportive resources
  • If the resident is in distress or the alleged perpetrator is known, inform PSOD immediately
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Arrive on scene and discuss options/needs for the survivor: Wellness Exchange / NYU Shanghai Counselor on Duty; Medical Care; Campus Safety/Local Police; Academic & Housing Accommodation
  • Apprise NYU Shanghai Counselor on Duty and decide if Counselor should come on scene
  • Contact AOD immediately if you need further assistance
  • Inform the survivor of privacy guidelines
  • If medical attention needed or survivor chooses to seek care, accompany to SinoUnited or Shanghai East Hospital
  • If students wish to gather evidence to press charges, accompany to local police station
  • Offer to change the survivor's room assignment if they don't feel comfortable — consult AOD before making any changes
  • Write detailed incident report and submit in Advocate
  • Follow up with responding RAs to process the experience and verify all information is confidential
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Ensure survivor is safe by verifying assistance in changing phone numbers, room assignments, email addresses has been offered
  • If alleged perpetrator is on-campus resident, discuss with Dean of Students whether temporary suspension, relocation, no contact orders, and/or PNG is warranted
  • Report to NYU Global Title IX Liaison in New York via email before 9 AM next day
  • Report incident to Campus Safety
Special Role of the First Responder: Speak softly and gently. Communicate acceptance and caring. Assure the survivor they are not alone. If survivor is hyperventilating, provide reassurances of safety and help them regain control of their breathing. Encourage the survivor to make small decisions as a way of helping regain control. Do not press the survivor to reveal unnecessary details of the assault. Listening is the most important skill you will use when intervening with a survivor of sexual assault.
Weapons
Prohibited items in university housing: Firearms, BB guns, ammunition, fireworks, explosives, other combustibles, swords, paintball guns, hunting knives, and prop guns.
If Threatened With a Weapon
RA / First Responder
  • For your safety, DO NOT resist or struggle
  • Keep a calm voice and demeanor
  • If possible, discreetly move bystanders/residents away from the area
  • Leave the area as soon as possible and contact Campus Safety
  • Contact Professional Staff on Duty
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Establish a safe location to meet with Campus Safety and staff
  • Stay in communication with Campus Safety until situation is resolved
  • Contact AOD
  • Arrange follow-up support or debriefing for staff/residents affected
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Discuss with Dean of Students to determine next steps
  • Discuss with Dean of Students if immediate suspension from housing is warranted
  • Assess situation and determine whether police should be involved
  • Ensure a post-incident debrief and communication plan
If Weapon Possession Is Reported
RA / First Responder
  • Maintain personal safety — DO NOT confront student
  • Clear bystanders from the area if it can be done safely
  • Get as much information as possible: identity of student, type of weapon, its location
  • Contact Campus Safety immediately and Professional Staff on Duty immediately
  • Submit detailed Advocate report by 9 AM next day
Professional Staff on Duty (PSOD)
  • Contact AOD
  • Keep in contact with Campus Safety until situation is resolved
  • Coordinate emotional support/debrief for staff or residents involved
Administrator on Duty (AOD)
  • Work with Campus Safety and PSOD to coordinate removal of weapons and/or confront students
  • Assess situation and determine whether police should be involved
  • Discuss with Dean of Students or Director of University Community Standards if immediate suspension from housing is warranted
  • Oversee official university communications — staff should not share details with residents or media
